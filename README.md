# Car Showroom

Car Dealership / Hire Purchase Management System for showrooms operating in Mombasa and across Kenya.

Built as a Frappe app. Phase 1 (this release): Core Inventory & CRM
- Dealership Branch / Yard
- Vehicle master (with images, documents, cost/profit calculation, auto stock numbering)
- Vehicle Inspection (checklist-based)
- Salesperson
- Showroom Customer
- Vehicle Lead + Lead Activity
- Test Drive (with double-booking prevention)
- Vehicle Quotation

Later phases: Sales & Cash Flow, Hire Purchase Engine, Collections & Payments (M-Pesa), Operations & Reporting, Public marketplace.

## Installation
    bench get-app car_showroom /path/to/car_showroom
    bench --site your-site install-app car_showroom
